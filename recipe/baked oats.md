## Ingredients

- 4 cups old-fashioned oats
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 4 cups milk
- 3 eggs
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1/2 cup of pecans or sliced almonds


Step 1
    In large bowl, stir together oats, brown sugar, baking powder and salt.
    
Step 2
    In large bowl, beat milk, 2 of the eggs, melted butter with whisk. Add milk mixture and raisins to oat mixture; gently stir to combine.
    
Step 3
     Pour into baking dish; let stand 15 minutes while oven preheats (350)
     
 Step 4
	 Bake 40 to 50 minutes or until center is set. Let stand 15 minutes before serving.
