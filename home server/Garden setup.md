 - Big Jim Chili pepper
 - Marigold

 - keystone resistanct giant pepper

### General garden plan
 - Start peppers and marigolds in seed tray
 - direct sow arugula
 - plant seeds 1/4 inch deep in soul when planted

### Plant Positioning
 - 8 sectors
 - each plant within it's own container in the sector
 - peppers do not share a sector with other plants
 - marigolds in between/around edges to deter pests
