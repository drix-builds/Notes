# Impossible Sausage, Egg & Cheese English Muffin

**Total: ~335 kcal · 31g protein · 2g fiber**

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Calories | Protein | Fiber |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English muffin | 1 whole | 130 kcal | 5g | 1g |
| Impossible sausage patty | 1 patty (~45g) | 110 kcal | 9g | 1g |
| Egg, whole | 1 large | 70 kcal | 6g | 0g |
| Reduced-fat cheddar | 1 slice (~19g) | 25 kcal | 4g | 0g |
| Cooking spray or ½ tsp butter | — | ~15 kcal | 0g | 0g |
| Salt & pepper | to taste | — | — | — |

---

## Instructions

1. **Cook the sausage.** Heat a skillet over medium. Cook the Impossible patty 2–3 minutes per side until browned and heated through. Set aside.

2. **Cook the egg.** Wipe the skillet, add a light coat of cooking spray or a small amount of butter over medium-low. Crack the egg in and cook to your preference — fried, over-easy, or scrambled all work. Season with salt and pepper.

3. **Melt the cheese.** Lay the reduced-fat cheese slice over the egg in the last 30 seconds of cooking, cover with a lid to melt.

4. **Toast the muffin.** Toast the English muffin to your liking while the egg finishes.

5. **Assemble.** Layer the sausage patty, then egg and cheese, on the bottom half of the muffin. Cap it and serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Sodium** is the one thing to watch — Impossible patties and reduced-fat cheeses both run higher on sodium than their conventional counterparts. If that's a concern, look for a lower-sodium Impossible variety or use a slice of fresh mozzarella instead of processed cheese.
- **Protein-to-calorie ratio** is strong here. At 31g protein for 335 kcal, this holds up well as a high-satiety breakfast without a lot of fat overhead.
- **Fiber bump:** swap the regular English muffin for a whole wheat one (+2g fiber, ~same calories) to push total fiber to ~4g.
- **Cheese note:** reduced-fat slice calories vary by brand — anywhere from 25 to 60 kcal. Check the label and adjust accordingly.
